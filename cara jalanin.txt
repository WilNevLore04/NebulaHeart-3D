conda activate space_gesture

Step 2: Pindah ke folder project NebulaHeart-3D menggunakan perintah:

cd "C:\Users\ASUS\OneDrive - Bina Nusantara\DOCUMENTS\1CV-CURICULUM VITAE\project\NebulaHeart-3D"
